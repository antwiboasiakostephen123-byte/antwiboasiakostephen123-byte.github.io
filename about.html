About the Event

Security is a shared responsibility. Many individuals underestimate its importance, leaving themselves vulnerable to both physical and cyber threats.

This program, in partnership with Gold Refinery Hall, raised awareness and provided practical training during the Akwaaba Night Celebration. We educated participants on personal safety, digital protection, and corrected common misconceptions about cybersecurity.

The program combined festive celebration with practical learning through interactive sessions, live demonstrations, and real-life examples of cyber threats such as phishing, scams, and unsafe digital practices.

Our objective was to promote vigilance, responsible digital behavior, and increased awareness of cybersecurity in everyday life while blending it beautifully with the rich Ghanaian cultural spirit of Akwaaba Night.
