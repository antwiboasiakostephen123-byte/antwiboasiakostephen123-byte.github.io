March 21, 2026 | Blending Ghanaian Culture with Cybersecurity Awareness

Welcome to Our Event

On March 21, 2026, Gold Refinery Hall successfully organized the Akwaaba Night Celebration, bringing together participants for a vibrant cultural experience.

As part of this event, our CY381 Cybersecurity team collaborated with the organizers to deliver a Security Awareness Program aimed at educating participants on both physical and digital safety.

The program combined festive celebration with practical learning through interactive sessions, live demonstrations, and real-life examples of cyber threats such as phishing, scams, and unsafe digital practices.

Our objective was to promote vigilance, responsible digital behavior, and increased awareness of cybersecurity in everyday life.
