About the Event

On March 21, 2026, Gold Refinery Hall successfully organized the Akwaaba Night Celebration. As part of this vibrant cultural event, our CY381 Cybersecurity team delivered a Security Awareness Program focused on both physical and digital safety.

We combined festive celebration with practical education through interactive sessions, live demonstrations, and real-life examples of cyber threats such as phishing, scams, and unsafe digital practices.

Our goal was to promote vigilance, responsible digital behavior, and increased awareness of cybersecurity in everyday life.

Key Resources & Takeaways

  • Always verify unknown callers before sharing information
  • Never forward suspicious messages without checking
  • Use strong, unique passwords + enable 2FA
  • Be cautious when using public Wi-Fi
  • Cybersecurity is about protection, not exploitation
